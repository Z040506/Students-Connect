Community Guidelines

Make this a welcoming campus space.

These suggested guidelines support respectful participation in the SCon demo.
  1. Be respectful. Treat students and organizations with courtesy, even when opinions differ.
  2. Keep it relevant. Share campus information that helps people connect and participate.
  3. Protect privacy. Do not post someone’s personal details, images, or contact information without permission.
  4. Be accurate. Check dates, schedules, and claims with the responsible organizer before sharing them as confirmed.
  5. Credit others. Acknowledge the people and organizations behind creative work and achievements.
  6. Help keep spaces safe. Report harmful or misleading content through the official channel for the page or group.

SCon is a front-end project demonstration. These guidelines are informational and are not an official university policy.
