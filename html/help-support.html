Help & Support

A little help for finding your way.

Quick answers about using this SCon.
How does My Profile work?

My Profile lets you save demo profile details in this browser. It is not connected to a student account.

Where do saved posts go?

Use Save on a post. It will appear under Saved Posts on this browser and can be removed there.

Are event dates and registrations official?

Dates shown in this project are sample information. Confirm current schedules and sign-up steps with the relevant organizer.

Do likes and comments reach other students?

No. Like, comment, and share controls are front-end demonstrations and do not publish to a service.

How can I report an issue?

Contact the SCon project team through the school or organization channel that shared this demonstration.
